Cozy Apartment in Lazurowy Dwór – Sztutowo


Perfect choice for a seaside holiday 🏖️


Welcome to a bright and cozy apartment located in Lazurowy Dwór in Sztutowo – a picturesque village on the Vistula Spit. This is an excellent spot for a relaxing holiday and a great base for exploring beautiful Baltic beaches and numerous regional attractions. The atmosphere is perfect for both family trips and romantic getaways.


Comfort & Apartment Amenities 🛏️


The apartment has been designed with guest comfort in mind. It features a spacious, air-conditioned room with a separate sleeping area equipped with a comfortable double bed. There is also a sofa bed in the living area. You also have at your disposal a modern bathroom with a shower and a balcony – perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation.

The apartment is equipped with everything needed to prepare meals: refrigerator, microwave, electric kettle, and full tableware. For maximum comfort we provide a TV, free Wi-Fi, air conditioning with heating function, wardrobe, iron and ironing board. The bathroom includes a starter set of cosmetics, fresh bedding, towels, hairdryer and clothes dryer.


Facilities at Lazurowy Dwór & Surroundings 🏊


As guests of Lazurowy Dwór, you can use the exceptional relaxation area which includes an outdoor swimming pool (open June–September), jacuzzi, salt cave and dry sauna(*). For children there is a playground, and barbecue facilities are available. Free private parking is provided on site.

The location is very convenient – nearby you will find grocery stores (Biedronka, Dino) and the narrow-gauge railway station. Just around the corner is the local gem – "Ptyś" pastry shop, famous for the best ice cream and sweets on the entire Spit 🍨.


Practical Information & Additional Services 📋


Check-in is possible from 4:00 PM, check-out until 10:00 AM. During the restaurant's opening period, breakfast can be ordered at the reception(**). Our apartment is pet-friendly – we accept dogs for an additional fee of 15 PLN per night (please inform us in the booking notes). The price includes all utilities, bedding, towels and a starter set of cosmetics and cleaning products.


* The wellness area is managed by an external company independent of p20apartament and availability may change. All information available at reception(**) or by phone.
** Reception and restaurant are operated by an external company independent of p20apartament and usually function during the summer season (June–September).

Location & Attractions

Top attractions in Sztutowo and the surrounding area

Sztutowo is an ideal base for exploring the Vistula Spit. Nearby you will find beautiful Baltic beaches, nature reserves, a narrow-gauge railway, and numerous cycling and hiking trails. It is a perfect place for active relaxation surrounded by nature!

  • Sztutowo Beach
  • Stutthof Museum
  • Vistula Spit Canal
  • Krynica Morska Lighthouse
  • Żuławy Narrow-Gauge Railway
  • Cycling and Hiking Trails of the Vistula Spit
  • Local restaurants and fish fry shops
