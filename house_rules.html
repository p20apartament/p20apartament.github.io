House Rules
P20 Apartment in Sztutowo

Dear Guests!
Please read the following rules carefully. Acceptance of these House Rules is a condition for making a reservation and staying at P20 Apartment.

Last updated: February 20, 2026

§ 1 General Provisions

  1. The P20 Apartment is rented on a nightly basis. Check-in time is from 4:00 PM on the day of arrival, and check-out is by 10:00 AM on the following day.
  2. Making a reservation (via Booking.com or direct contact) constitutes acceptance of these House Rules.
  3. Upon check-in, the Guest is required to present a valid identity document (ID card or passport). The owner reserves the right to refuse check-in in case of lack of ID, intoxication, or aggressive behaviour.
  4. Unregistered persons may not stay in the apartment between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM.
  5. The Guest may not sublet or hand over the apartment to third parties without the owner’s written consent.
  6. The owner may refuse to accommodate a Guest who has seriously violated these rules or caused damage during a previous stay.
  7. In case of serious breach of the rules (noise, property damage, aggression), the owner has the right to terminate the agreement immediately without refund.

§ 2 Booking and Cancellation Policy

  1. Bookings are made through the Booking.com platform (via “Book now” buttons on the website) or directly with the owner (email / phone).
  2. All booking conditions, including cancellation policy, prepayment, deposit and payment terms, are set by Booking.com at the time of booking. Please read them carefully before confirming your reservation.
  3. For direct bookings (outside Booking.com), the owner sends a confirmation email with bank account details for prepayment / full payment.
  4. Prices shown on the website and in the confirmation are gross prices (including 8% VAT). VAT invoice is issued upon request made during booking.
  5. The owner does not require a refundable security deposit. Booking.com may require a deposit in selected cases – this information is displayed during the booking process.
Important: All cancellation rules, refunds and prepayment conditions are governed by Booking.com according to the selected rate plan (flexible / non-refundable / partially refundable). The owner has no influence on these terms.

§ 3 What is included in the price

  1. The price includes:
    • full set of bed linen and towels (extra change available for a fee)
    • final cleaning (standard level of dirt)
    • utilities (electricity, water, heating, Wi-Fi)
    • access to complex facilities (pool, jacuzzi, sauna, salt cave – subject to availability and opening hours)
  2. Additional paid services (to be arranged with the owner):
    • pet stay (dogs/cats) – 100 PLN for the entire stay (only with prior approval)
    • breakfast at the complex restaurant (during its opening season – price according to restaurant menu)
    • mid-stay cleaning
    • extra bed linen / towel change during longer stays

§ 4 Guest Obligations

  1. The Guest is obliged to:
    • observe quiet hours (10:00 PM – 6:00 AM)
    • immediately report any malfunctions or damages
    • close windows, turn off lights and appliances when leaving the apartment
    • not bring additional persons beyond the number declared in the booking
  2. It is strictly prohibited to:
    • smoke tobacco / vape inside the apartment and on the balcony
    • organise noisy parties or gatherings
    • move furniture, drill holes, stick anything to walls
    • use heaters, electric radiators or high-power devices not provided in the apartment

§ 5 Contractual Penalties and Liability

  1. Smoking violation – 500 PLN
  2. Violation of quiet hours / organising a party – 500 PLN
  3. Loss / damage to key / access card – 200 PLN
  4. Damage / loss of bed linen, towels, textiles – from 150 PLN per item
  5. False activation of fire alarm – 500 PLN + possible fire brigade call-out costs
  6. Leaving the apartment after 10:00 AM without agreement – 150 PLN for each started 30 minutes
  7. In case of major damage, the owner reserves the right to claim compensation under civil law in addition to contractual penalties.

§ 6 Complaints

  1. Complaints regarding the stay should be submitted in writing to: ijstaszewscy@gmail.com within 14 days of check-out.
  2. Complaints are processed within 14 working days.

§ 7 Final Provisions

  1. Matters not regulated by these House Rules are governed by Polish Civil Code and relevant tourism service laws.
  2. Any disputes shall be resolved by the court competent for the owner’s registered office (court in Gdańsk).
  3. The House Rules may be amended – reservations made before the amendment are subject to the version in force on the date of booking.
  4. In case of force majeure preventing the stay, the parties shall not be liable for non-performance.

We wish you a pleasant and relaxing stay!
