4. Cookies

4.1 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. They are used, among other things, to ensure proper functioning of the Website, analyse visit statistics and remember cookie consent preferences.

4.2 Types of cookies used on the Website

  • Necessary / functional – enable basic operation of the site, navigation, cookie banner (session or locally stored)
  • Analytics – Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager (_ga, _gid, _gat, _gcl_aw etc.) – collect anonymised information about website traffic

4.3 Managing cookies

You can manage cookies at any time through:

  • the cookie banner displayed on your first visit
  • your browser settings (blocking or deleting cookies)

Disabling analytics cookies does not prevent use of the Website, but may affect our ability to analyse statistics.

5. Data retention period

  • Google Analytics data – default 26 months (can be changed in GA account settings)
